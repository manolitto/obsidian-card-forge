pathfinder-2e-actions.woff2
============================

Icon font containing only the Pathfinder 2nd Edition action symbols,
keyed to the Unicode points used by the Obsidian "pf2-action-icons"
plugin and the Fantasy Statblocks layout:

  U+2B32 ⬲  Reaction
  U+2B3A ⬺  Two actions
  U+2B3B ⬻  One action
  U+2B3D ⬽  Three actions
  U+2B53 ⭓  Free action

Source
------
Extracted from the base64-embedded TTF in
https://github.com/thiagocoutinhor/pf2-action-icons (MIT for the
plugin code) and converted to WOFF2 with `fontTools`. The font itself
was originally produced by IcoMoon as "Pathfinder 2e Action Glyphs"
under the Paizo Community Use Policy.
