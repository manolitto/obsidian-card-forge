# Icons — MINI D20

The 25 SVG icons in this folder come from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/)
and are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence
(CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). They are unmodified
apart from normalisation to a single path filled with `currentColor`, so the
card's ink colours them.

CC BY 3.0 requires attribution to the artist. Anyone who publishes or shares
cards rendered with these icons must keep this attribution.

## Artists

- **Delapouite** — https://delapouite.com/
- **Lorc** — https://lorcblog.blogspot.com/
- **sbed** — http://opengameart.org/content/95-game-icons
